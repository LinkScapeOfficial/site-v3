## 3 Roles and Responsibilities

Each role in the LinkScape organization carries specific responsibilities:

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| CEO & Co-Founder | Organizational vision and strategic direction; final decision authority; external relations and partnerships; Founder/Co-founder veto per Ordinance s.13. |
| Co-Founder & CFO | Financial stewardship; Hack Club Bank relationship and reporting; budgeting, expense approval and compliance calendar oversight. |
| CTO | Technical direction and architecture decisions; compute resource governance (80x H100 fleet); platform security and access control. |
| Project Maintainers | Project-specific decisions, code review, and team coordination. |
| Members | Contributions, issue resolution, and community participation. |
| Fellows | Alumni and senior contributors providing mentorship, technical review, and advisory input. Non-fiduciary, no operational authority. |
